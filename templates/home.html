About Phishing:

Phishing is a cyber attack using fraudulent emails, messages, or websites to steal personal and financial information.

Attackers impersonate trusted organizations to trick users into revealing passwords, credit cards, or account details.

Common signs include suspicious sender addresses, urgent requests, spelling errors, and links to duplicate websites.

Always verify URLs before clicking and enable two-factor authentication for critical accounts.

Use PhishSence tools to check suspicious links and emails before engaging with them.
